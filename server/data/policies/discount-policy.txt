RETAIL STORE DISCOUNT POLICY

1. Discounts up to 10% may be suggested for slow-moving products.

2. Discounts above 10% require store owner approval.

3. Discounts should not be recommended for products that are experiencing high demand or low stock unless the store owner explicitly requests it.

4. The agent should consider recent sales and current inventory before recommending a discount.

5. The agent must not apply a discount automatically.

6. Any discount recommendation should clearly explain the reason for the recommendation.
