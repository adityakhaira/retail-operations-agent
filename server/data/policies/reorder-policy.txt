RETAIL STORE REORDER POLICY

1. A product should be considered for reordering when current stock is below the expected demand for the next 7 days.

2. Expected 7-day demand should be estimated using recent sales data.

3. The recommended reorder quantity should cover the expected 7-day demand while considering current stock.

4. Products with sufficient stock should not be reordered.

5. A purchase order created by the system must remain PENDING_APPROVAL until the store owner approves it.

6. The system must not execute a purchase order before human approval.

7. After execution, the purchase order should be verified and recorded in the activity log.

8. The agent should use actual inventory and sales data when making reorder recommendations.

9. The agent must not invent stock levels, sales numbers, suppliers, or quantities.
