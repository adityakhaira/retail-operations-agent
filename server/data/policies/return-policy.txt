RETAIL STORE RETURN POLICY

1. Customers may request a return within 7 days of delivery.

2. Products must be unused and in resalable condition.

3. Products that are damaged after delivery by the customer may not qualify for a return.

4. The store owner may review exceptional return requests manually.

5. Refund decisions should not be automatically executed by the AI agent.

6. The agent may explain the return policy but should not claim that a refund has been issued unless the store system confirms it.
