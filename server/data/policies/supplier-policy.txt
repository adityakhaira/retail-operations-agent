RETAIL STORE SUPPLIER POLICY

1. Products should be ordered from their assigned supplier whenever supplier information is available.

2. The agent should identify the supplier associated with a product before creating a purchase order.

3. Supplier contact information may be shown to the store owner when needed for operational purposes.

4. The agent must not invent supplier names or contact information.

5. A purchase order may contain products from multiple suppliers.

6. Supplier orders are considered simulated in this prototype unless an external supplier integration is explicitly connected.

7. The system must not claim that a real supplier was contacted when only a simulated execution was performed.
